Ethan Liu
Abstract: Ethan is a practicing Data Analyst at the US Department of State and an MS Systems Engineering student at the George Washington University. Ethan applies data engineering techniques to automate data collection and enginneer data infrastructure, which he uses to create new, optimize existing, and retire legacy systems. Noably, in fiscal year 2025, the data insights provided by his regional Automated Passports Production Tracker enabled the Department to consistently service over 500 in-person passport applicants per day in the Los Angeles region.

In his academic research, Ethan is developing high-fideltiy orbits propagation software to model how the pre-event states of multiple dynamic spacecraft collectively influence the propagation of their post-event orbital elements. He poses that legacy mission architecture systems must modernize to consider events with multiple dynamic bodies, without approximation.
